Overview

Video generators can improve their own output.

Video generators often produce videos that are visually appealing but physically implausible. Proprio allows them to score and enhance the physical plausibility of their own outputs at inference time.

Proprio measures how the generator responds to controlled changes in a video’s latent representation. Smaller, more stable denoising residuals indicate that a sample better fits the dynamics the generator has learned. The same signal can help select among candidates, refine a sample, or do both.
